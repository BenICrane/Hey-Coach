Plan from 26 Oct 2026 to 3 Jan 2027, built from [Principles.md](Principles.md) and the 2026 goal in [Yearly goals.md](Yearly%20goals.md): **build aerobic base up to 80 km per week by the end of the year**.

Weeks run Monday to Sunday. Volumes are running km only.

### Guidelines

- At least 80% of running time in HR zones 1–2. Strides twice a week are the only intensity.
- 5 runs a week in Blocks 1–2, rising to 6 in Block 3.
- Long run no more than about 30% of weekly volume.
- One strength session a week.
- Tuesdays have LMS from 17:30, so run in the morning or rest.
- Take a recovery week early if easy runs drift above HR 150 at about 6:00/km, or if a niggle lasts to the next morning.
