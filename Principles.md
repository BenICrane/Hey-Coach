# Principles 
### Training blocks 
* Training blocks are the basic units of training. They should be between 2 and 4 weeks long. 
* Each training block should have a specific focus: 
    * Improving endurance/aerobic capacity
    * Improving VO2 max
    * Improving lactate threshold
    * Increasing time at race pace 
* Each training block should be 75% progressive overload, and 25% recovery. 
* The purpose of the training block should become more specific to the race, the closer to the race it is. 

### Overall volume 
* Overall, 80% of volume should be in zone 2 
* When increasing volume, aim for 10% increase per week. Maximum of 15% per week.
